Why Nelavista

Built around the real African student experience.

Students often have to jump between WhatsApp groups, Google, YouTube, PDFs, random websites, separate learning platforms, job boards, and campus information, just to get through a single semester.

Nelavista is bringing these experiences together, into one platform built for how African university students actually study, learn, and move forward.

Founder

Built by a student who understands the problem.

I built Nelavista because I experienced the same fragmented university life we're trying to fix: searching for materials, preparing for exams, learning skills independently, and trying to figure out what comes after graduation.

Nelavista is my attempt to build the platform I wish I had as a student.

Abdul Afeez Tella
Founder & CEO, Nelavista
