Settings

Your control center for how Academia works for you. Every setting here actually does something — nothing on this page is decorative.

AI Tutor

Response depth, teaching approach and difficulty apply everywhere the tutor explains something to you — Ask Nelavista, the AI Tutor chat, and the Explain/Summarize/Quiz actions on Materials and Topics. Academic context and conversation continuity apply to Ask Nelavista and the AI Tutor chat specifically, since the Materials/Topics actions are single questions, not a conversation.

Response depth
How much detail the tutor gives by default.
Teaching approach
How the tutor structures explanations.
Difficulty
How much background knowledge the tutor assumes.
Use my academic context
In Ask Nelavista / the AI Tutor chat: let the tutor see your university, faculty, department and level, and tailor examples to them.
Use my previous conversations
In Ask Nelavista: let the tutor recall your recent questions there for continuity across visits. Off starts every conversation fresh. (The AI Tutor chat always has real conversation threads regardless of this toggle — this only affects Ask Nelavista's shorter Q&A history.)
Anything else the tutor should know about you?
Optional. Shared with the tutor on every question — e.g. "I learn best with real-world examples" or "I'm preparing for a transfer exam."
Study & Exam Preferences
Preferred practice mode
Which Mock Exam mode is pre-selected when you start practicing (you can always switch on the day).
Automatically explain wrong answers
When reviewing a practice attempt, fetch Nelavista's explanation for every wrong answer automatically instead of tapping "Explain" each time.
Notifications
Mock exam results
Get a notification (bell icon, top right) when a CBT practice attempt is graded.

That's the only notification Academia can send today. Study reminders, course/material updates and community activity aren't wired up to any notification yet — we're not going to show you a toggle for something that can't actually notify you.

Appearance
Theme
Applies everywhere — Academia, AI Tutor, Materials, Mock Exam, Community, Profile, Settings. "System" follows your device's light/dark setting automatically.

Language: Nelavista is currently English-only. Yoruba, French and Hausa are planned, but no screen is translated yet — we'd rather ship that honestly than offer a language picker that doesn't change anything.

Privacy & Data

Nelavista keeps three genuinely different kinds of data about you — worth being precise about which is which:

Learning progress
Topics you've completed and Mock Exam scores — your real academic record. View it on your Dashboard or Practice History. Not cleared from here — it isn't personalization data, it's what you actually did.
AI Tutor conversation history
The questions you've asked Ask Nelavista and its answers. Used for the "previous conversations" continuity toggle above.
AI personalization memory
The teaching-style preferences and "anything else you should know" text above. Reset it from Danger Zone below, or edit it directly in AI Tutor settings.
Download your data
A copy of your profile, preferences, AI Tutor history, mock exam attempts and topic progress, as a JSON file.
