Straight talk

What we do, and what we don't.

Nelavista Talent is a professional network, not a job board. Organisations come to us with real work, and we introduce people whose evidence fits.

We will

  • Help you build skills through structured tracks
  • Record your assessment results and reviewed work
  • Show organisations your profile only if you choose to
  • Introduce you when your evidence matches a real brief

We won't

  • Promise you a job for signing up
  • Mark a check as passed before you have passed it
  • Share your profile without your consent
